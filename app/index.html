    The road to the platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10. No trophy is online and none needs a second run — but keep a save at the start of each chapter, because there is no mission replay.

    Saves: the game gives 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), then rotate the remaining 5 for free-roam cleanup.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy on the whole list can be missed — and even that one is still unconfirmed. Everything else can be cleaned up in free-roam after the story.

    Nothing else is missable

    • Optional missions that disappear do not affect any trophy; there are far more than the 100 the checklist wants.
    • Quest givers who go quiet mid-story — Kenton, for example — all come back later, and their quests stay open after the story.
    • Fences that disappear do not matter for trophies; new fences unlock as you go.
    • A few magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that later close, but they also spawn elsewhere and are not needed in full.
    • Time-sensitive quests with an hourglass icon fail after a while, but none of them is tied to a trophy.
    • Strangers & Freaks strands stay in the world after the story.

    What is still unconfirmed

    One trophy, Bad Luck, I Guess, has no confirmed trigger yet — the best current lead is the Payday Loans store from the prologue, and its guide says so plainly instead of guessing. The Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude is also still unconfirmed as a requirement; the guide tells you to do it anyway, because it is the only chance you get.
