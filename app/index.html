        Roadmap to the platinum

        Built from the official descriptions only: the order below follows what each trophy's own wording demands — story unlocks first, strands and activities in the middle, the long counters and the money last. Nothing here needs a second playthrough if the two missable warnings in stage 1 are heeded.

        The list at a glance

        • 55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 are hidden on PlayStation until earned.
        • 21 story and strand trophies carry the campaign; the chapter trophies and the final mission make up the spine.
        • The long counters: 250 stunts, 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles discovered, 50 challenges, 20 different races, 42 Shooting Range challenges to gold.
        • The money trophies: $250,000 laundered in Marked Bills, $100,000 spent in shops, and every garage property bought.
        • A Long Road (100% completion) is the second gold and the usual last trophy before the platinum, Vice Got a Price!

        Missables — read this before the story

        Only two trophies on the official list read as potentially missable, and both are flagged from their own descriptions, not from rumour: one is about choices, the other about a second attempt. Neither is confirmed missable by the official list — the protection is simply cheap: rotating manual saves, and letting scripted failures happen.

        Every other trophy on the list is worded as a completion, a counter, a collection or a repeatable robbery or race — all still earnable after the story, as far as the official descriptions state.

        Save, export & settings

        Your checklist lives in this browser's local storage — nothing leaves the device and the app works fully offline. Export it to a small JSON file before switching browsers or clearing site data, and import it back afterwards.

        Settings

        With the shield on, an un-earned story trophy shows as a hidden entry — grade and kind only — in the checklist and in its guide pane. Earn it, and its name, description and guide appear.

        About this guide

        Every guide here is written for this entry in our own words. Names, descriptions and grades are the official ones, as published for PlayStation and listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles. Where a guide draws on another guide, wiki or forum, that source is named beside the trophy — a source is cited, never copied. Where the official description does not pin a detail down, the guide says so instead of inventing one.
